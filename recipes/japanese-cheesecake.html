Japanese Cheesecake

Description

A light and fluffy cheesecake known for its soft, airy texture. It has a mild creamy flavor and is less heavy and sweet than traditional cheesecake.

Ingredients

For the Cheesecake:
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 50g unsalted butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 6 large eggs, separated
  • 60g all-purpose flour
  • 20g cornstarch
  • 120g white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
For the Topping:
  • Powdered sugar (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 320°F (160°C).
  2. Melt the cream cheese, butter, and milk together over low heat. Stir until smooth, then let it cool slightly.
  3. Separate the egg whites from the egg yolks.
  4. Add the egg yolks and vanilla to the cream cheese mixture and mix well.
  5. Sift in the flour and cornstarch. Mix until smooth.
  6. In another bowl, beat the egg whites and lemon juice until foamy.
  7. Slowly add the sugar while beating until the egg whites form soft to medium peaks.
  8. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the cream cheese mixture in several portions.
  9. Pour the batter into a lined cake pan.
  10. Put the cake pan inside a larger baking tray and add hot water to the larger tray.
  11. Bake at 320°F (160°C) for about 20 minutes.
  12. Lower the temperature to 285°F (140°C) and bake for another 40-50 minutes.
  13. Turn off the oven and leave the cheesecake inside with the door slightly open for about 15 minutes.
  14. Remove the cheesecake and let it cool.
  15. Dust with powdered sugar before serving if desired.
