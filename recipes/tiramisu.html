Tiramisu

Description

A classic Italian dessert made with coffee-soaked ladyfingers and layers of smooth mascarpone cream. It is finished with cocoa powder for a rich coffee and chocolate flavor.

Ingredients

For the Cream:
  • 250g mascarpone cheese
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream
  • ⅓ cup white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
For the Coffee Layer:
  • 1 cup strong brewed coffee or espresso, cooled
  • About 200g ladyfinger biscuits
For the Topping:
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • Chocolate shavings (optional)

Steps

  1. Brew the coffee and allow it to cool completely.
  2. Beat the heavy cream and sugar until soft peaks form.
  3. Add the mascarpone cheese and vanilla.
  4. Gently mix until the cream is smooth and thick.
  5. Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the cooled coffee. Do not soak them for too long.
  6. Arrange one layer of dipped ladyfingers in a dish.
  7. Spread half of the mascarpone mixture over the ladyfingers.
  8. Add another layer of coffee-dipped ladyfingers.
  9. Spread the remaining mascarpone mixture on top.
  10. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4-6 hours.
  11. Dust the top with cocoa powder before serving.
  12. Add chocolate shavings if desired.
